Workspace ownership

Ownership is a default guide, not a fixed permission boundary.

Workspace Lead role Support / intent
Review room Case chair Case chair leads the interpretation flow; Case coordinator keeps actions moving.
Phenotype review Clinical reviewer Clinical reviewer leads phenotype and HPO review, with counselor or external consultant input when needed.
Variant review Bioinformatician + Laboratory scientist Bioinformatician and Laboratory scientist lead variant interpretation, ACMG/AMP criteria, and validation evidence.
Hypothesis board Case chair + Core board Core board records positions; Case chair facilitates the current diagnostic direction.
Evidence inbox Case coordinator Case coordinator triages incoming evidence with reviewer input from clinical and bioinformatics roles.
Review history Case coordinator + Case chair Case coordinator and Case chair keep the timeline, decisions, and exported artifacts readable.
